Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air outside all shift long, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Broken Arrow HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we schedule visits around your operating hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical concerns for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with written service records that show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. You get detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, keeping your kitchen cooking and guests comfortable in the meantime.

Inspections & Documentation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify correct venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. Findings go into a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Broken Arrow

Every hour without climate control costs a business money. In a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. In a retail store, it sends shoppers home on a summer afternoon. For a server room, it means overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Broken Arrow HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Broken Arrow and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: we handle all of it on rooftop and split systems. Our first priority is pinpointing the fault and getting heating or cooling back as quickly as possible. After that, we put maintenance and monitoring in place to prevent a repeat, and each response is fully documented for your records.
